## Chapter 1

### Workflow Automation
- AI can automate repetitive tasks for both users and businesses, such as booking restaurants, processing invoices, managing customer requests, and entering data.
- Many automated tasks require AI to interact with external tools.
- AI systems that can plan and use tools to complete tasks are called **AI agents**.

## Planning AI Applications
- Foundation models make it easy to build AI demos, but building a useful and profitable AI product is much harder.
- Before developing an AI application, developers should consider why the product should be built and how it can provide real value.

## Use Case Evaluation
- Before building an AI application, it is important to understand why it should be built.
- Companies may adopt AI to avoid competitive risks, improve profits and productivity, or explore how AI could affect their business.
- After identifying a valuable use case, companies should decide whether to build the AI solution in-house or buy an existing solution.
