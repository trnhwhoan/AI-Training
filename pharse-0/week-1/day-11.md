### Data Organization
#### Data increare
- Nowadays, smartphone's users will be take photo and record video, the companies will record everything about product, employee and customer. Each year, have billions contracts to create.
- Image, video, log and PDF is **unstructured and semi-structured data**. So, organizing all this data in a way that allows for future retrieval is crucial.
  **-> data too much -> need good organizing -> look and use easy.**

### How AI organizing?
- AI can supporting this work.
- AI can auto:
  - Create describe by text for image and video.
  - Connect queries by text with fit images.
  - ex: Google Photos used AI for looking and display these fit images with looking query. Google Image Search can create image if without fit image.

#### AI + Data Analysis
- AI can write program to:
  - Create **data visualization**
  - Identify **outliers**
  - Show forecast, example is revenue forecast.
    - Sales data -> AI -> chart, outliers and revenue forecast.

#### Unstructured -> Structured Data
- Company can use AI to **export semi-structured data** -> organizing and looking data is easy.
- ex: Simple examples: Extracting information from credit cards, Driver's licenses, Invoices, Tickets, Contact information from email signatures.
