## Conversational Bots
- Conversational bots are versatile AI applications that can help users find information, explain concepts, brainstorm ideas, and practice conversations.
- They can also act as companions, simulate different personalities, and support different types of social interaction.
- In research, groups of conversational bots can even be used to simulate societies and study social dynamics. This shows that conversational AI is not limited to simple question-answering.
- For businesses, one of the most common applications is customer support. AI-powered support bots can respond to customers faster and help companies reduce costs while improving the customer experience. 
- AI can also work as a product copilot that guides users through difficult or confusing tasks, such as filing insurance claims, doing taxes, or looking up company policies.
- Conversational AI is not limited to text interfaces. Voice assistants such as Google Assistant, Siri, and Alexa are examples of conversational agents that use voice. AI-powered 3D characters are also becoming useful in games, retail, and marketing.
- One important gaming application is **smart NPCs**. Traditional NPCs are usually scripted with a limited set of actions and dialogues.
- AI can make NPCs more intelligent and allow them to respond more dynamically to players, potentially enabling new types of interactive games.

## Information Aggregation
- People need to filter and process large amounts of information, but keeping up with emails, messages, news, and documents can easily become overwhelming.
- Generative AI can help by **aggregating, filtering, and summarizing information**. According to Salesforce's 2023 Generative AI Snapshot Research, 74% of generative AI users use it to distill complex ideas and summarize information.
- AI can therefore act as an information assistant that collects information from different sources and turns it into concise and useful summaries.
- AI can process and summarize large amounts of information from documents, websites, emails, and research papers.
- A common use case is **"talk-to-your-docs,"** which allows users to ask questions and retrieve information from their documents conversationally.
- For enterprises, AI can aggregate information from meetings, emails, and Slack, then extract important facts, open questions, and action items.
- AI can also help analyze customers and competitors.
