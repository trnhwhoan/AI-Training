### Model Evaluation Metrics
#### MAE - Mean Absolute Error
- Indicates, on average, by how many units the prediction deviates from the actual value.

  <img width="212" height="86" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/292d8c19-daaa-497f-b4ef-647b006a9803" />

#### MSE - Mean Squared Error
- Penalize big predictions heavier.

  <img width="238" height="84" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/22508c3a-bc01-466c-8218-ed4d1f2d4bb1" />

#### R^2 - Coefficient of Determination
- Measure level the model explain variation of target data.

  - R^2 = 1 - perfect prediction
    
  - R^2 = 0 - not better baseline average prediction
    
  - R^2 < 0 - maybe worse that baseline

*R^2 not % exactly prediction.
