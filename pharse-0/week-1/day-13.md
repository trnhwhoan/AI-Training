## Book: Grokking Machine Learning
## Chapter 1

### The Role of AI and Humans in the Application
- The role of AI in an application affects how the product should be designed and developed. 
AI features can be classified as **critical or complementary**, **reactive or proactive**, and **dynamic or static**.
- If AI is critical to the application, it must be highly accurate and reliable because the product cannot function properly without it. 
- Reactive AI responds to user requests, while proactive AI provides information or actions without being directly requested. 
- Dynamic AI features can continuously adapt to users and their data, while static features are updated periodically.
- The role of humans is also important. **Human-in-the-loop** means involving humans in AI decision-making. 
- Instead of fully automating a system immediately, companies can gradually increase automation using the **Crawl-Walk-Run** approach. As AI becomes more reliable, human involvement can decrease.

## AI Product Defensibility
- AI products also need **defensibility**, meaning they should have advantages that make them difficult for competitors to copy or replace. This is especially important because foundation models make AI applications easier to build, but they also lower the barrier for competitors.
- Another risk is that foundation models may improve and eventually include features that were previously provided by separate AI applications. Therefore, an AI product should provide value beyond simply adding a layer on top of a foundation model.
- Three important competitive advantages in AI are **technology, data, and distribution**. Data can become a strong advantage when companies use product usage information to understand users, identify weaknesses, and continuously improve their products.
